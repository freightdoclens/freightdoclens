The problem

Every shipment generates a stack of documents. Today most freight forwarders and customs brokers still re-type them by hand.

15+

documents per shipment

Bills of lading, invoices, packing lists, customs forms and certificates, each in a different layout.

Hours

of manual data entry

Teams copy container numbers, HS codes and values into ERPs and customs systems by hand.

Costly

errors and delays

One wrong digit can mean a held container, demurrage fees or a customs penalty.

How it works

Three steps from a scan to data in your system.

1

Upload

Drop in a PDF, photo or scan. Phone photos, faxes and skewed pages are fine.

2

Extract

The model reads the page, identifies the document type and pulls out every key field.

3

Review and export

Low-confidence fields are flagged for a quick human check. Export clean JSON or CSV to your TMS or ERP.

Built for real-world documents

Not just clean PDFs. FreightLens is designed for the paperwork that actually arrives.

Multilingual

Handles English, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Spanish and more, including documents that mix languages.

Messy scans

Works on low-resolution, rotated, faded or photographed pages.

Stamps and overlays

Reads text even when official stamps and signatures cover it.

Handwriting

Picks up handwritten corrections and notes, and marks them for review.

Confidence and validation

Every field has a confidence score. Container numbers and HS codes are checked against format rules.

Source highlighting

Click any extracted value to see exactly where it came from on the page.
